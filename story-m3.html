5-Day Cross-Country Trip with M3: Performance & Practicality

BMW M3 Competition • 1,200 km Roadtrip • Published by Minh

Driving a high-performance sports sedan on a long road trip is often seen as a compromise between thrilling dynamics and daily comfort. Over five days and 1,200 kilometers across diverse terrain, the BMW M3 Competition was put through its ultimate test.

First Impressions & Highway Comfort

Starting out early in the morning, the highway stretch allowed us to test the adaptive suspension and cruising capabilities. In Comfort mode, the M3 behaves surprisingly civilized, making it an capable long-distance companion.

Mountain Passes & Raw Performance

The true character of the M3 shines when switching to Sport+ mode on winding mountain roads. The precise steering, immediate throttle response, and relentless power output made every curve an adrenaline-filled experience.

Final Verdict

The BMW M3 Competition proves that you don't need to sacrifice everyday usability for high-end track performance. It remains one of the most complete all-rounder drivers' cars on the market today.
